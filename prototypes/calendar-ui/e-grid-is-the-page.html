Still wrong in the app

1 · The grid ran past the fold in the APP — measured, then fixed. The claim this round was built on was arithmetic on the shipped classes ("88px of two toolbar rows plus six 104px cells ≈ 828px in an 800px window"), and on inspection it was wrong about the mechanism: the shipped cell was a min-h-[72px] minimum, so what actually pushed the month down was a busy day growing its cell, not the cell being tall. Measured in Chromium at 375×812 with a busy September fixture: busiest day cell 182px, grid 867px, page 1111px — 291px of month below the fold, and 55px more month than the screen is tall. Fixed 2026-10-03 under #128 gap 2, which also fixed the false claim this note originally rested on (see the CSS comment above the cell rules: a min-height is not a height, and a clip is not a cap). The four boxes above are this page's own live numbers, not the app's.

2 · By person does not exist yet. The rows in the Filters sheet are this page's answer to mark 1.11. The shipped calendar has no per-member filter, no member data on this surface, and nothing to persist a choice to. It is an idea, not a port.

Round 3 — what changed from D
  • The rail is gone at every width. Not collapsed, not stacked under, not behind a hamburger at 1100px. There is no `
    `, and there is no media query that could bring one back.
  • The grid is the page. One column, full width, at 375px and at 768px. At 768 there is no mini month competing with it, because there is no mini month.
  • Filters is a button that opens one sheet holding Calendars and By person, at every width. Mark 1.4.
  • Overdue left the surface. Due tasks ride the day they are due. Mark 1.13.
  • Day cells stop growing at the app's own breakpoint. 72 → 92 → 104, so a 640px phone is never handed a 104px cell and a 768px tablet is never handed six of them. Mark 1.14.
  • One date control, the shared one. Label · Today · ‹ › in a single pill — the DayNav construction, not a fourth hand-built pager. Marks 1.16 + 1.17.
  • Search is row two, full width, at every width, with the shortcut printed on it. Mark 1.15.
What was given up, and where it went
  • Jump to a date without the picker. A rail mini month was one glance. Cost: one tap. The label opens the picker, and the grid is right there.
  • The next five events at a glance. Cost: you leave the calendar. It is on the dashboard, which is where "what now" is answered.
  • 14% of grid width at 1280px. D's own "still open" note. Gone — the grid takes the width instead of sharing it.
