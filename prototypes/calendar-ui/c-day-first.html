The risk — say this out loud

This changes what the page is. Power users who open the app to scan the month now scroll past a hero every visit, and the “September 2026” month grid moves below the fold on a laptop. The mitigation is to make the hero band collapsible and remembered (localStorage, like resolveInitialView already does for views) so it costs one click for those who don't want it.
