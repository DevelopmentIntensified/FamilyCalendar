What this adds
  • Filters as a destination — 5 calendars + per-member “by person”, with counts. Today colour is only discoverable via tooltip.
  • Up next — the five next events, always. Answers the question people actually open the app to ask.
  • Overdue-task glance in the rail, not buried on the Tasks page.
  • Search (⌘K) across events, people and places.
  • Mini-month doubles as the month picker — the toolbar popover disappears.
What it costs

A new CalendarRail.svelte (mini-month, filters, up-next) plus a useCalendarFilters store. Month grid stops being full-bleed so cells shrink ~14% at 1280px — that is the one real trade-off: fewer chips per cell on smaller laptops. Everything else in prototype A still applies.

Responsive

Below 1100px the rail becomes a left drawer behind Filters. Below 640px the month grid switches to the existing 3-chip + action-sheet pattern unchanged — the whole app keeps today's mobile behaviour.
