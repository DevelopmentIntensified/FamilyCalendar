Round 2 — what changed here
  • The key is now true. It said four words and drew four swatches that matched no chip on the grid. It now enumerates the four kinds the app actually ships, and every line is built from the same chipMarkHTML() the grid renders from — so a claim cannot outlive its chip.
  • Search moved to the toolbar. “Move this to be on top of the calendar in the bar with everything else.” The rail keeps the mini month, up next, calendars, by person and overdue tasks.
  • Both are prototype-only. The shipped calendar has no search and no calendar filter — those blockers are landing, and this page shows them as missing rather than faking them (ground rule 7).
