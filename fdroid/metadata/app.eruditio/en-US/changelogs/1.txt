Eruditio 1.0

First release of the friction-plus-reading app:

- Pause screen over chosen scroll apps (~10 s, breathing, explicit continue/replace)
- 30 bundled public-domain classics with exact position memory
- Reading sessions record check-ins; completed segments generate review cards
- On-device spaced repetition with mastery levels, decay, and mastery challenges
- Progress view: texts read, concepts by mastery, reviews due, weekly interceptions
- Daily streak for completed sessions, displayed separately from mastery
- Post-session handoff suggestions (Khan Academy / Exercism)
- Theme picker (Paper, Ink, Sepia, Terminal)
- One-time purchase unlocks the core loop; debug builds bypass
- No accounts, no ads, no trackers
