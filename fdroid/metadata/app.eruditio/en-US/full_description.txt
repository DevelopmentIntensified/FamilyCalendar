Eruditio stands between you and your most-opened scroll apps. When a target app opens, a
brief friction screen appears: a short delay, a breathing moment, and an explicit choice —
continue into the app, or take a learning session instead.

Choosing the session opens a quiet, offline reading surface bundled with 30 public-domain
classics (Plato, Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, KJV selections, Spurgeon sermons, Augustine,
missionary biographies). Completed sessions feed an on-device spaced-repetition review
schedule with honest mastery levels (familiar / proficient / mastered), decay when
neglected, and a daily streak kept strictly separate from mastery metrics.

Privacy is the architecture, not a claim: no accounts, no sync, no ads, no trackers —
zero third-party SDKs. All progress lives on the device. The app costs once; future
content tracks arrive as optional packs. Never a subscription.

Eruditio uses the standard app-usage access API to detect when a chosen target app opens.
It never reads your browsing, never hard-blocks an app, and records only what you choose
at the pause screen.
